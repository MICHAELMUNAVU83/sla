How much does a consultation cost?

Your first consultation is free. We use it to understand your matter, outline the realistic options, and give you a clear estimate before any engagement begins.

How are your legal fees structured?

We offer hourly, fixed-fee and contingency arrangements depending on the matter. Whichever applies, you get the structure in writing before we start.

How long will my case take?

The duration of a legal case varies significantly based on its complexity and whether it goes to trial. We prioritize efficiency and will provide you with a realistic timeline and regular updates on the progress of your matter.

What should I prepare for our meeting?

Bring any contracts, correspondence, court papers and a short timeline of events. Nothing is required up front — we can work from whatever you have.

Integrity, clear communication and tireless advocacy — backed by a 98% client success rate across complex legal matters.
